Membership

Join 800+ customer education leaders from 50+ technology companies.

Who can join

Membership is open to training executives, managers, and professionals on a management path at vendors in information and communication technology, including hardware, software, cloud, and learning technology companies.

We also welcome trade associations and professional bodies whose work supports our members. The board may decline an application where there is a conflict of interest.

Membership is held by your company, so every employee has access to this website and all members-only content: research, survey results, SIG meetings, and more.

If your company is already a member and you need an account, ask your company’s primary contact to email community@cedma-enterprise.org and we’ll add you.

Read the Member Code of Conduct

Company membership

One membership covers every employee in your company. Contact us for details.

  • Website and members-only content for every employee
  • Research, surveys, and Pulse of the Industry
  • Ask the Members: put a question to the whole membership
  • A searchable archive of survey results, conference presentations, planning tools, and newsletter articles
  • Two seats at each conference, including accommodation, meals, and refreshments
  • All Special Interest Groups and workshops
  • CTTM certification
