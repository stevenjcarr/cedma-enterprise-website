Our partners

Our partners help keep membership affordable and make the conferences possible. Our annual partners support CEdMA Enterprise all year, and we thank the partners who supported the Autumn Conference 2026.

Annual partners

Annual partner

Kryterion

Kryterion delivers online and in-person certification exams through its Webassessor platform, with psychometric services to support program design. It has worked with credentialing bodies and technology companies since 2001, and is part of the Drake International group.

Visit website
Annual partner

Skillable

Skillable provides hands-on virtual labs that let customers learn complex software and AI tools by using them. Since 2004, more than 400 companies have used it to onboard users and build product proficiency.

Visit website
Annual partner

Certiverse

Certiverse is an AI-assisted platform for developing and delivering certification exams, with one vendor covering every phase. It is designed so teams of any size can create psychometrically sound exams without deep testing expertise.
