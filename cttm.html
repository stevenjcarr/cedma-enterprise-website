Certified Technical Training Manager

CTTM is the credential for education leaders that covers the commercial and business skills needed to run a training function.

How the program works

CTTM is built on the book Technical Training Management, available from the publisher, BCS, and from Amazon. You may already have a copy; if not, you will need one before you start.

Each cohort works through six sessions, grouped into three pathways. Before each session you get self-study modules based on the relevant book chapters, and the sessions themselves are interactive, with polls and discussion. If you miss one, the recording is there to catch up.

At a glance

Format
Six live virtual sessions (Zoom)
When
Second Thursday of each month, 4pm UK time
Length
60 to 90 minutes per session
Recordings
Every session is recorded
Course book
Technical Training Management

Six sessions, three pathways

Each pathway covers two sessions. At the end of each pathway, a short professional discussion confirms you understand it, and you earn that pathway's badge.

From enrollment to certification

  1. 01

    Prepare

    Get the book and work through the self-study modules for each session.

  2. 02

    Attend six sessions

    One live, interactive session a month, recorded in case you miss it.

  3. 03

    Earn pathway badges

    A professional discussion after each pathway confirms your understanding.

  4. 04

    Prepare for the exam

    A dedicated exam prep session once all three pathways are complete.

  5. 05

    Take the exam

    Choose from regular remote exam sessions, then receive your CTTM badge.

Who it is for

New managers

Recently promoted into leading an education team and want grounding in the business side.

Established leaders

Already running a team and want a recognized credential for skills built on the job.

Future managers

Senior practitioners preparing to move into a management role.
