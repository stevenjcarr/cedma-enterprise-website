Special Interest Groups

Small groups of members who meet online to work through one area of customer education in depth.

How the groups work

Each group meets online at 4pm UK (11am Eastern, 8am Pacific), led by a member volunteer. A short presentation opens the discussion, and members often bring real documents. Groups are open to all members, and each group's past meetings are available to browse.

Certification

Next 22 Oct 2026

For the people responsible for their organization's high-stakes certifications. Topics include test development, scoring approaches, and test security, among many others.

Education Design and Development

Last met 7 Oct 2026

Compares best practice in training design and development, especially the course development metrics and KPIs that matter to a training organization and its business model. It also covers e-learning, both self-paced and live, with all its operational aspects and formats.

Training Partners

Next 26 Nov 2026

Reviews the role of training partners in reselling and delivering training, from partner selection criteria to partner training, certification, and ongoing knowledge maintenance, plus the lessons of running a global partner network.
