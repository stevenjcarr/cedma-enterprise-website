Awards

We recognize the organizations and individuals who have moved customer education forward, through two annual awards, special awards for outstanding individuals, and our Emeritus Board Members.

Annual awards

Presented each year

Annual award

Innovation Award

Recognizes a creative approach to solving a problem on a real-world project. A successful outcome matters less than the approach, but it must be a project that was actually carried out, not just an idea.

Organizations

Annual award

Impact Award

Recognizes a program that has made a clearly measurable impact, such as return on investment, attach rate, revenue, or student satisfaction.

Organizations

Special awards

Presented for exceptional contributions

Special award

Service Award

Recognizes outstanding service to CEdMA Enterprise and its members. Many members deserve it, so the board recommends one particularly outstanding individual when appropriate.

Individuals

Special award

Lifetime Achievement Award

Recognizes an individual whose dedication and contribution over many years have been central to the success of CEdMA Enterprise.

Individuals

Emeritus Board Members. Board members who serve more than two terms (over six years) receive an Emeritus Board Member recognition badge.

Lifetime Achievement Award · 4 November 2025

Mike Dowsey

A special award, given to recognize someone whose dedication and impact are felt throughout everything we do at CEdMA Enterprise.

Mike keeps everything running smoothly. He is our problem-solver and our linchpin, always making sure CEdMA Enterprise succeeds, and the foundation on which countless achievements have been built.

He never chases recognition, yet his influence guides us all. For his tireless dedication, his invaluable contributions to our success, and for being the backbone of CEdMA Enterprise, the board was honored to present Mike with this award at the Autumn Conference 2025.
