About CEdMA Enterprise

The definitive professional community for customer education leaders in global technology companies, run by volunteers from member companies.

What is CEdMA Enterprise?

CEdMA Enterprise (the trading name of Computer Education Management Association Europe Limited) is the premier organization for training executives, managers, and professionals on a management path in hardware and software companies. Through CEdMA you can draw on the experience and resources of members who share a passion for high-quality training and certification on technology products, while running an efficient business unit.

Running customer education inside a technology company is its own discipline. You build courses and certifications for fast-moving products, run a business unit with revenue targets, and prove value to the rest of the company. Few people outside the field understand that combination.

From the start, members have worked on a simple principle: our companies are not competing for the same learners, so we can share our successes and failures openly. That non-competitive sharing of ideas is still at the heart of the association.

Today CEdMA Enterprise brings together more than 800 people from over 50 global technology companies.

Vision, mission, and goals

Our vision

To be the premier professional organization for our members: an evolving store of professional training experience that presents a positive and productive image of our industry, and gives members outstanding support through mentoring, professional development, and networking.

Our mission

To be the most effective advocate for training management at every level of the technology training industry, as a vital part of advancing member companies' objectives. We do this by maximizing value to members through the support and services essential to their professional development, and by educating member companies, members, and others on current and future training trends.

Our goals

  • Educate member companies, members, and others on issues and trends in the technology training industry
  • Support members through meetings, communications, publications, programs, and services
  • Build a proactive, growing membership
  • Keep the association financially stable for the future

Our history

1993

CEdMA Europe is founded by training managers from Oracle, Software AG, IBM, Sun, Sybase, Novell, and WordPerfect.

2024

CEdMA Europe becomes CEdMA Enterprise. As a company-oriented trade association, the new name reflects our focus on larger global enterprises.

2026

A new identity, built on what members told us they value most.
